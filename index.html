OUR STORY

Named after the café dog who runs the place

Set inside a former boiler station just off the tourist streets, Café Luis pairs genuinely excellent specialty coffee and matcha with a rotating art gallery downstairs. Luis, the resident dog, greets every guest personally — and regulars will tell you the vegan desserts and rhubarb cake are worth the trip alone. A warm, LGBTQ-friendly space that's become one of Gdańsk's most-loved hidden gems.
